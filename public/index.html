    Go script guide

    Upload one .go file implementing Decide. It returns your food request and energy contribution for each day.

    agent.go

    View, Action and the runner are supplied by SOMAS. Do not declare them or add main().

    Inputs: View

    Decide runs once per living agent per day, before anyone acts. Return both choices from this opening snapshot.

    Fields supplied in View
    Field Type Meaning
    Round int Current day, from 1 to the run’s day limit (up to 50).
    PatchFood int Carrots in the field before anyone harvests.
    Energy int Your energy after today’s sunlight, up to the chosen capacity.
    AgentCount int Living agents, including you.
    LastFood int Your actual previous harvest; initially 0.
    LastEnergy int Your actual previous contribution; initially 0.
    Seed int64 Your repeatable random seed for this day.
    Inputs for configurable gardens

    Put // somas:api=2 above package main to use these additional integer fields.

    Pantry is your stored food. DailyFood is your supper requirement. Sunlight is the energy you gained today. FieldCapacity and EnergyCapacity give the field and wallet limits.

    Price quotes a new carrot at dawn. LockedPrice and Pending give the price and energy already deposited for a partly planted carrot. A zero lock means the converter is empty.

    LastEaten counts yesterday’s bites. TotalFood, TotalEnergy and TotalEaten are your lifetime harvest, contributions and meals measured in carrots eaten.

    Actions and rules

    Food 0–10

    Carrots requested.

    Energy 0–24

    Energy offered to farming.

    Both choices are integers. Harvests are limited to ten carrots and the available field stock. Agents can give up to the energy in their wallets.

    • Dawn. Sunlight tops up each living amoeboid’s wallet to its capacity. Unspent energy stays there.
    • Morning. Amoeboids harvest in shuffled order. Carrots go into their own pantries.
    • Afternoon. Amoeboids plant in a separately shuffled order. The first energy token locks a carrot’s price until it grows. A full field or too few carrots to plant stops deposits.
    • Evening. Each living amoeboid eats its daily requirement, or everything it has if it falls short. A short meal means starvation. Garden setup controls whether leftovers carry over.
    • Choose 1–50 days in Garden rules. The default is 30. The garden ends after the last supper or when every amoeboid has died.

    Uploading

    • One .go file, up to 20 KB, using package main and func Decide(v View) Action.
    • Helper functions, package variables and standard-library imports are allowed. Third-party packages and network access are unavailable.
    • Return comfortably under a second; game decisions have a 1.2-second response deadline.

    Name your file’s strategy and upload it to the public library. In Garden setup, choose it for any amoeboid. Each copy keeps its own memory.

    Memory and randomness

    Package variables persist between days, privately for each selected slot. They reset for each new game. Use LastFood and LastEnergy to track actual outcomes.

    With import "math/rand", use rand.New(rand.NewSource(v.Seed)) for repeatable choices.

    Checks, errors and playback

    Uploading compiles the file and tests a first decision. A later crash or timeout stops that agent and displays an error; failed decisions use zero actions.

    Run computes the game before playback. Pause and speed affect the animation. Saved replays keep their original decisions.

    Go uploads require hosted mode with an isolated runner. You can still download these examples.

    Uploaded code is public.

    Keep passwords and private keys out of your file.
