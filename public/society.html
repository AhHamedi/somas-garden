FIELD GUIDE

A society, one day at a time.

The teams write the behaviours. The world keeps the rules.

01

Stay alive. Find satisfaction.

Agents choose where to live, how much to harvest and how much to eat. Extra carrots earn diminishing satisfaction. Thinking converts mind into satisfaction, one for one.

02

Decide whether to organise.

Governments start with zero rules. Members spend mind to propose and vote on quotas, taxes, storage, officers and more. Passed decisions take effect the following day.

03

Cooperate across plots.

Federations can unlock donations, shared records and movement rules. Authorised representatives cast one combined vote per member plot.

04

Begin again, with experience.

Every month the meteor resets the world. Only each individual's private memory survives. Whether it learns from that experience depends on its team's code.

You are the observer.

Inspect the whole world, or switch an individual to its own view to see what it actually knows. The controls change playback, never an agent's decisions.
