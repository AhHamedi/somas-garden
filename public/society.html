Simulation rules

Agents choose where to live, harvest, eat and think. Extra food earns diminishing satisfaction; thinking earns one satisfaction per mind spent. Governments start with zero rules. Votes can introduce quotas, taxes, officers and other powers. Decisions activate the following day. Federation representatives act for their plots. A meteor resets the world each month, retaining only private memories.
