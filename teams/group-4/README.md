# The Organisation: Dialists (v3)

One strategy with four dials, one per teammate:

- Entrepreneurial 1.0: found a government, elect a Trade rep, switch to ranked harvest.
- Power 0.1: don't chase offices, back whoever is nominated.
- Generosity 0.5: give half of any food above today's need to Trade. Trade sends it to the plot with the biggest blight shortfall.
- Restraint 0.4: once the field drops to 70%, push for a quota of daily need + 2. Vote against tighter or looser quotas.

Harvest is today's need plus our share of overnight regrowth, never above the quota.

Tested locally on 10 seeds against Wardens, Locusts, Envoys, Barons and Sunchasers (our team's mean satisfaction per month / everyone's survival):
